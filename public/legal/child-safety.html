Child Safety & CSAE Policy

18+ only. No minors. No exceptions in V1. Under DPDP a child is anyone under 18; COPPA protects under-13s; the FTC’s NGL order (July 2024) banned anonymous messaging for under-18s; Google Play (26 Aug 2026) age-restricts anonymous/random chat; Apple (Feb 2026) says random/anonymous chat does not belong on the App Store. Omegle shut down (Nov 2023) under this exact pressure. We built for that reality.

1. Prevention

  • Neutral DOB age gate + explicit 18+ confirmation, server-enforced (checkbox alone is rejected; mismatched/failed attestations are logged).
  • No public profiles, no directory, no friend requests — random ephemeral matching only.
  • Text-first posture: video is P2P with pre-connection warnings; sexual-content prohibition is shown before first chat.
  • Minimal analytics, no behavioural ads to anyone, no profiling of minors (prohibited under DPDP Art.9, GDPR/DSA Art.28, UK Age Appropriate Design Code).

2. Detection

  • Real-time text signals for grooming/CSAM/sextortion (critical tier) + sexual/threat/hate/scam tiers; decision metadata retained, content not stored.
  • Hash-matching readiness (PhotoDNA / NCMEC / StopNCII) for any uploaded/retained evidence; human review for every critical hit.
  • Rate limits + behavioural signals (mass handshakes, rapid Next, spam bursts) to catch bots and sextortion farms.

3. Response — emergency workflow (staff must follow exactly)

  1. Report received (in-app reason child-safety/emergency, email to tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com subject [CHILD-SAFETY], or auto-flag).
  2. Classify within minutes; treat as critical until cleared.
  3. Preserve evidence — session metadata + report held 180 days minimum; place legal hold (no deletion).
  4. Restrict offender — disconnect, long-hold ban, block rematch.
  5. Child-safety escalation — notify child-safety lead + legal immediately.
  6. Mandatory reporting — POCSO cases to Indian authorities; US-apparent CSAM/enticement/trafficking to NCMEC CyberTipline per 18 U.S.C. §2258A; other regions per local duty (UK OSA, AU eSafety, BR protections).
  7. Victim care — do not re-victimise; no internal viewing beyond need-to-know; offer reporting resources.
  8. Account action + audit — document every step for the transparency report.

Failing to report suspected child sexual abuse can itself be an offence (POCSO). Ordinary support staff must not improvise — escalate.

4. Contact

Child-safety lead, grievance, law enforcement (all regions): tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com — use subjects [CHILD-SAFETY], [GRIEVANCE], [LAW-ENFORCEMENT] (monitored continuously; emergencies prioritised).

Play-ground truth: Google Play Child Safety Standards self-certifications + child-safety contact point are completed before any Play release; iOS ships only if the product is redesigned so random/anonymous chat is not its primary experience.
