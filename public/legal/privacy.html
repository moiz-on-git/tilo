Privacy Policy

Who we are: Tilo, India. Contact (support, privacy/DPO and grievance): tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com.

Tilo is an 18+ only, anonymous-to-other-users, ephemeral random chat service (text + WebRTC video). We do not require your real name. We do not store chat text, voice or video by default. Other users see only “Stranger”. The platform processes minimal technical data for safety, security and legal compliance.

2. Why we process (purpose limitation)

Service operation · safety & fraud prevention · moderation · legal compliance (DPDP, IT Act/Rules, CERT-In, POCSO, tax) · support · minimal reliability analytics. No behavioural advertising. No sale of personal data.

3. Consent & withdrawal (DPDP)

Before first match you see an itemised notice and give free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous consent. Withdrawing is as easy as giving: Privacy → Withdraw consent in-app, or POST /api/consent {"consent":false}. Withdrawal stops matching; safety/legal-hold records are retained per Retention Schedule.

4. Children — 18+ only

Anyone under 18 is a child under DPDP/IT Rules and may not use Tilo. There is no parental-consent flow in V1 — under-18 access is refused (see Child Safety Policy). Do not use Tilo if you are under 18.

5. Sharing

Cloud/hosting (India region), WebRTC STUN (connection metadata only), safety tooling — all under written DPAs. Law enforcement: only on valid legal process via tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com (see Law Enforcement Policy). No data sale. EU/UK transfers use SCCs/IDTA where applicable.

6. Retention

Live media: never stored. Session IDs: short-lived. Security (ICT) logs: 180-day rolling in India (CERT-In). Removed-content/report evidence: 180 days (IT Rules) or longer on legal hold. See full matrix.

7. International transfers

Indian-user data is hosted in India. If you use Tilo from the EU/UK/US/AU/BR, your ephemeral traffic may transit signalling/CDN infrastructure; safeguards (SCCs/contractual) apply. Region allow-listing is enforced — unavailable regions get HTTP 451.

8. Your rights

  • India (DPDP): access, correction, erasure, nomination, grievance redressal (~90-day rights workflow). Use in-app Privacy → Download/Delete my data or GET /api/data-export / POST /api/data-delete.
  • EU/UK (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection. Contact tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com. Breach notice to authority within 72h where required.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): know/delete/correct, opt-out of sale/sharing (we do not sell/share). No discrimination for exercising rights.
  • US under-13 (COPPA): service is 18+; under-13 data is not knowingly collected — accounts/sessions are refused and purged.

9. Automated moderation

Real-time text screening (child-safety critical signals, sexual explicit, threats, PII/scam) runs as classify → retain decision metadata only. Critical hits end the chat and create a safety hold. You can appeal via Reporting & Appeals.

10. Security (no blueprint)

Encryption in transit (HTTPS/WSS), hashed identifiers, access controls, monitoring, backups, 6-hour CERT-In incident escalation, tested breach playbook. “Encrypted in transit” ≠ “platform-blind E2EE” — we process signalling/safety metadata for moderation and legal compliance.

11. Grievance & changes

Grievance Officer details and SLAs: Grievance Policy. Material changes are announced in-app and versioned (consent version bump).
