Grievance Redressal Policy (India)

Grievance Officer (India, IT Rules): Grievance Officer — Tilo · tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com · India. For all regions (support, privacy/DPO, child safety, law enforcement): tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com — mark subject lines [SUPPORT], [PRIVACY], [CHILD-SAFETY], [LAW-ENFORCEMENT], [GRIEVANCE].

  • Acknowledge every complaint within 24 hours with an ack ID.
  • Resolve within 7 days (IT Rules Feb-2026 update; Grievance Appellate Committee appeal within 30 days).
  • Illegal-content removals (IT Rules r.3): act within 36 hours.
  • Emergency content (private-area nudity, sexual acts/conduct, impersonation): disable/remove within 2 hours of the specified complaint.
  • Government/court takedown (Feb-2026 amendment): disable within 3 hours.
  • DPDP data-principal requests follow a documented ~90-day workflow; grievance responses are tracked to closure.

How to complain

In-app: Privacy → Grievance / Contact · API: POST /api/grievance {"category","description","contact"} · Email above. Include ack/session IDs, timestamps, and what remedy you seek (removal, ban, correction, apology, data action).

Significant Social Media Intermediary (SSMI) readiness

Below 5M Indian registered users we operate as an intermediary with RGO + published rules/privacy/user agreement. If we cross 5M we will appoint a Chief Compliance Officer + Nodal Contact Person and publish monthly compliance reports (complaints received/action taken, proactive removals, government orders).
