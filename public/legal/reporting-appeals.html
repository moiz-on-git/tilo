Reporting & Appeals (notice-and-action you can use in one tap)

1. Report in-app

Every live session shows Report · Block · Next · Leave. Tapping Report opens reasons: child-safety, nudity/sexual, harassment, threat, scam/fraud, privacy violation, impersonation, illegal content, spam, other, emergency. The report auto-attaches session metadata (no media stored by default) and returns an ackId. Emergency/child-safety jumps the queue.

2. Report outside the app

Email tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com with subject [GRIEVANCE] (general) or [CHILD-SAFETY] (minors/CSAE) with: what happened, when (IST/UTC), session/ack ID if any, and screenshots only if lawful to share. Do not send CSAM to us — describe it and let authorities guide evidence.

3. What happens next (SLAs — IT Rules Feb-2026)

  • Acknowledge within 24 hours.
  • Emergency nudity/private-area/impersonation: disable within 2 hours.
  • Illegal-content removals: 36 hours. Standard: resolve within 7 days; Grievance Appellate Committee appeal within 30 days.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery (US TAKE IT DOWN Act, FTC enforcing from May 2026): remove within 48 hours of valid notice.

4. Appeal a moderation decision

Reply with your ackId to tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com within 30 days. Include why the decision was wrong and any context. A different reviewer re-examines; outcomes: uphold / reduce / lift + expunge strike. Child-safety critical holds are released only after human + legal clearance.

5. Transparency

Aggregate counts (reports by reason, actions, government requests) are published via GET /api/transparency and periodic reports.
