Content Moderation Policy (how reports become actions)

Covers IT Rules due diligence (“reasonable efforts”, government/court takedown 3h per Feb-2026 amendment, illegal-content removals 36h, nudity/impersonation 2h), DSA notice-and-action (Art.16) + statements of reasons + trusted flaggers, UK OSA illegal-content duties, and app-store UGC duties (report/block/filter + support contact).

1. Pipeline

User → Age gate → Matchmaking + safety filters → Live ephemeral chat → Report/Block/safety signals → Risk classifier → Low (continue) / Review (human) / Critical (immediate action + evidence hold + legal escalation). AI never solely decides bans for critical cases — a trained human reviews.

3. What reporters get

Every report returns an ackId (e.g., via POST /api/report or in-app Report). Emergency reports show emergency SLA. Every enforcement shows a statement of reasons (what rule, what action, how to appeal).

4. Repeat offenders

Strikes accumulate per hashed network/device signal: default 24h × strikes; critical child-safety = long hold + manual review before any release. Block (one tap) prevents rematch in both directions. Appeals can lift or uphold; abusive appeals are logged.

5. Records

Removed content + associated records preserved 180 days for investigation (IT Rules) or longer on legal hold; registration info 180 days post-cancellation. Transparency aggregates published at GET /api/transparency and in periodic reports (SSMI monthly if >5M Indian users; DSA reports if EU in scope).
