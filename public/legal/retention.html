Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

Principle: ordinary session data ≠ reported evidence ≠ legal hold. We keep the minimum, for the shortest lawful time, then auto-delete. Exact periods are configured via env (ICT_LOG_DAYS etc.) and shown at GET /api/compliance/status.

Deletion

In-app Privacy → Delete my data or POST /api/data-delete queues erasure of non-essential records. Safety/legal-hold/tax records are exempt and disclosed as such (with the legal reason). Server clocks sync to government NTP so retention windows are auditable.
