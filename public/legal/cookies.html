Cookie & Tracking Policy — minimal by design

We do not use advertising cookies, Meta/TikTok pixels, session-replay, fingerprinting for ads, or cross-site behavioural tracking. That is deliberate: for an anonymous chat app, heavy analytics creates DPDP/GDPR exposure and poisons child-safety posture.

  • Strictly necessary: in-memory session/socket state, consent choice (localStorage: tilo-consent-v1), age-gate flag (session only), safety rate-limit counters. No expiry beyond session except consent memory.
  • Preferences: chat mode (text/video), camera/mute toggles — device-local only.
  • Analytics: none by default. If minimal reliability telemetry is ever added, it will be opt-in, documented here, and excluded for any suspected minor.

Withdrawing consent clears matching eligibility; clearing site data removes local flags. Questions: tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com.
