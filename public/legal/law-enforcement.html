Law Enforcement / Legal Requests Policy

Contact (all regions): tiloappcomplaints@protonmail.com — subject [LAW-ENFORCEMENT]. We verify every request before disclosing anything. Users: we are anonymous to each other, not to lawful process — hashed technical logs exist and may be disclosed where legally required.

1. What we need

  • Official agency email/domain + badge/case reference; court order / §69 IT Act direction / MLAT as applicable.
  • Specific identifiers (ack ID, hashed ref, timestamps with timezone) — no fishing expeditions.
  • Emergency disclosures (imminent death/serious harm, ongoing child exploitation): mark EMERGENCY with facts supporting urgency.

2. What we can provide

Minimal metadata only: hashed network identifiers, timestamps, report/moderation records within retention (180d). No chat transcripts or recordings exist by default — we cannot produce what we never stored. Preserved evidence is held per Retention Schedule.

3. Process & timelines

  • Acknowledge emergency requests promptly; standard requests within 72h.
  • Government/court takedown: disable within 3 hours (IT Rules Feb-2026 amendment); illegal-content removals 36 hours; nudity/private-area/impersonation complaints 2 hours.
  • Preserve on lawful hold even past routine deletion; notify the user unless legally barred (e.g., gag order, POCSO sensitivity).
  • CERT-In cyber incidents (breach/leak): report within 6 hours to CERT-In (incident@cert-in.org.in / 1800-11-4949); affected users + Data Protection Board notified per DPDP timelines (Board + users, 72h breach track; 90-day rights responses).

4. Cross-border

Foreign requests come via MLAT/treaty or to the Indian entity with local validity review. NCMEC CyberTipline reports flow immediately for apparent US-jurisdiction CSAM/enticement/trafficking (18 U.S.C. §2258A).
