Community Guidelines — zero tolerance, plainly stated

Tilo is 18+, anonymous, ephemeral. If you cannot follow these rules, do not tap Start.

Prohibited — immediate suspension + possible authority reporting

  • Sexual exploitation: CSAM in any form, grooming, solicitation involving minors, sextortion, non-consensual intimate imagery (TAKE IT DOWN Act: removal within 48h of valid notice), sexual interaction with minors, moving minors off-platform.
  • Sexual content: nudity, sexual acts/conduct on camera or in text, sexual solicitation. Random video is heavily filtered — assume it will be caught.
  • Harassment & harm: threats, stalking, repeated targeted abuse, doxxing (publishing another’s personal info), blackmail, hate speech, impersonation.
  • Illegal activity: credible threats, fraud/scams, trafficking, instructions/facilitation for serious crimes, obscene/pornographic/paedophilic material per IT Rules.
  • Privacy violations: recording/sharing intimate material without consent (IT Act s.66E), publishing phone/address/OTP/bank details.
  • Platform abuse: ban evasion, botting, spam, automated account creation, malicious links/code, deepfake impersonation without clear disclosure (IT Rules synthetic-media duties).

What happens when you break them

Message blocked → instant disconnect → strike → device/network restriction (24h default; child-safety critical: long hold + human + authority review) → evidence preserved 180 days → NCMEC/authorities notified where required. Repeat offenders are banned, not just IP-limited.

Safety tools (one tap, in every session)

Report (with reason) · Block (never rematch) · Next/Leave. Reports attach session metadata automatically. Emergency/child-safety reports jump the queue.
